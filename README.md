# BibleProject Interview Kit

Thank you for considering a role at BibleProject! We want you to know what to expect, so this guide walks you through our process, your role's take-home challenge, and the values behind our work.

Each role has its own folder under [`roles/`](roles/) with its take-home challenge and job description. The challenges reflect the type of work you'd encounter daily in your role. They are open-ended and deliberately ambiguous in places. We are just as interested in your thinking and approach to problem-solving as we are in your technical aptitude. Questions about the process are always welcome, and if a requirement is genuinely unclear, please ask.

## Interview Stages

| Stage | Time | What to expect |
| ----- | ---- | -------------- |
| Application | - | Start by applying through our careers page. We'll get to know your experience and how it connects with the role. |
| Phone screen | 30 min | Talk with a hiring manager about your experience, the role, and what you're looking for. This is a chance to ask questions, too. |
| Take-home challenge | about 4 hours | Explore a role-specific challenge inspired by our work. You'll have the flexibility to complete it on your own schedule. |
| Technical interview | 1 hour | Talk through your work with members of the hiring team. Tell us about your approach, the choices you made, and what you might explore next. |
| In-person | about 4 hours | Get to know the team you'd join and colleagues from across the organization. It's a time for you to ask questions, too. |
| Hiring team review | - | Your interviewers will come together to share perspectives and make a thoughtful decision. |
| References | - | We'll contact your references to hear more about their experiences working with you. |
| 1:1 with an executive leader | 30 min | Explore our mission and values, and ask any questions you still have. |
| Offer extended | - | Your hiring manager will walk you through the offer and answer your questions. Take the time you need to consider it. |
| Offer accepted | - | Welcome to BibleProject! We'll be in touch to help you get ready for your first day. |

## About BibleProject

[BibleProject](https://bibleproject.com) is a crowd-funded nonprofit that creates videos, podcasts, classes, and other tools to help people experience the unified story of the Bible that leads to Jesus. Every resource is available for free and most are localized into dozens of languages. The work is shaped by a paradigm that treats the Bible as messianic, communal, human and divine, ancient, unified, wisdom, and meditation literature - designed to reveal its meaning over a lifetime of re-reading and reflection.

### Mission and Values

BibleProject exists to help people experience the Bible as a unified story that leads to Jesus, so that his followers can be unified and contribute to shalom in the world.

- **Generous:** God is our generous host, so we have an abundance mindset.
- **Unifying:** God is creating a worldwide and diverse human family, so we serve as a catalyst for its unity.
- **Thoughtful:** God invites us to live by his wisdom, so we are both empathetic and strategic.
- **Creative:** God creates order out of chaos for life to flourish, so as his image-bearers, we innovate.
- **Humble:** God humbles himself by collaborating with humanity, so we treat every person as valuable.
- **Unhurried:** God rested in his work, so we toil sustainably and find rest.

### Product Principles

- **Fewer things, better:** We stay **focused** and keep things **simple**, saying no to things that distract from the mission.
- **Serve through iteration:** We work in rhythms of **continuous iteration** that empower us to **send our best work** to our audience.
- **Go for a long walk with a friend:** We find **space and time** for good ideas to emerge and do it **together**.
- **The Bible is the model:** We take inspiration from the Bible as a **thoughtfully designed**, **exceptionally crafted**, and **enduring** work of art made for humans.
