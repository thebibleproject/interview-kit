# Platform Engineer

BibleProject's platform engineers build and operate the shared services that product teams depend on: a GraphQL API, the numerous services behind it, and the infrastructure and developer experience that support them. The role is hands-on and collaborative, with significant architectural ownership and a strong emphasis on reliability, observability, and maintainable production systems.

## Take-home Challenge

Assume ownership of an existing media-processing service and extend it to deliver videos and derived assets to multiple partner-owned stores, including per-partner destinations, backfills, and restart-safe processing.
