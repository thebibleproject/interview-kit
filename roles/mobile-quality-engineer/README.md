# Quality Engineer

As a Quality Engineer at BibleProject, you empower teams to ship reliable, secure, and performant software by embedding quality practices throughout the development lifecycle. You lead with automation and observability, design for testability, and partner with Engineers, Product, and Design to shift quality left so that defects are prevented, not just detected. You'll design, build, and own the app's automation suite, identify where coverage is needed across the test pyramid and partner with engineers to close the gaps, and use production signals to target risk — bringing demonstrated experience driving quality for production software at scale.

## Take-Home Challenge

## Step 1: Code Challenge

Tasks

1. Fix the suite (~45 min): repair broken and flaky tests, explain each cause, and recognize one failure that’s a real app bug.
2. Add 2-3 tests (~60 min): based on the app’s functionality, choose the features you would prioritize. Follow the repo’s established patterns.
3. Build something new (~45–60 min): add a new capability, such as data-driven tests, a flakiness checker, or testing a device-specific capability such as accessibility, dark mode, or auto-rotate.
4. Report (~20–30 min):
    - Your reasoning for prioritizing the features you chose in Task 2
    - Bug reports for problems found in the tests or in the app
    - How you would plan to execute this suite in CI
    ** Do not use AI to generate this report, it should be your writing

## Step 3: Submit Your Assessment

Thank you for taking the time to complete the Mobile Quality Engineer Interview Take Home Assessment.

How to Submit Your Work

1. Start your own private repository from the challenge repo template.
2. Commit your work on top of that first commit. Please keep it private and don't open pull requests against it.
3. Once completed, share the private GitHub repository with @tiffanywilson, @lucasstorti, @jeffikus.
4. Reply to your Take Home Assessment email to confirm your submission, including the GitHub repo link.
We'll reply to confirm we've received your submission within a week and give you an anticipated timeline for review.

Note: At BibleProject, we value thoughtfulness and creativity. We see the value in AI tooling and don’t mind you using them to generate code for this assignment. If you choose to do so, please include with your submission details on what tools and models you used and how you chose to utilize them. Keep in mind that you should understand the changes you've made and be able to answer questions about the system. We do ask that any writing, such as your report, is done by you.
